Dostyk Refinery

Border transshipment on the Kazakhstan–China corridor.

Company overview

A connection between two rail systems.

Dostyk Refinery is a border-area petroleum transshipment facility near Dostyk station. Its infrastructure supports physical fuel handling between the Chinese and Kazakh railway gauges.

Group materials indicate up to 66,000 m³ of storage capacity. Modernization milestones and projected annual throughput require current confirmation before use in contractual representations.
