Sterling Terminal Almaty

A specialized fuel terminal in Almaty.

Company overview

Aviation fuel and petroleum-product storage.

Sterling Terminal Almaty is a group infrastructure company with 19,000 m³ of aggregate storage capacity. Materials show 10,000 m³ for Jet A-1, 6,000 m³ for RT and 3,000 m³ for TS-1.

The facility includes rail access, truck loading infrastructure and a laboratory. The materials cite 12 test types; applicable standards, accreditation and available services are confirmed through the facility’s documentation.
